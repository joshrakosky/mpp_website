About Metro Printed Products

One Source. Infinite Resources. Since 1990.

Founded in 1990, Metro Printed Products has built its reputation on strong relationships, personalized service, and a commitment to making our customers' jobs easier.

Over the years, we've experienced significant growth through both organic expansion and strategic acquisitions, strengthening our expertise, expanding our resources, and broadening the customers and industries we serve. Throughout that growth, we've remained committed to the values that built our business: responsiveness, accountability, creativity, and trust.

As an NWBOC-certified woman-owned business, we're proud of our independent leadership and the long-standing relationships we've developed. With the power of our preferred suppliers network, we combine the personal attention of a dedicated team with the capabilities to support businesses of every size.

Above All, Service Comes First.

While our capabilities, technology, and resources continue to grow, we believe the most important thing we provide is exceptional customer service.

To us, great service means responding quickly, communicating clearly, taking ownership, and following through on every commitment. We understand that our customers depend on us, and we take that responsibility seriously.
