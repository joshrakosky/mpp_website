Promotional Products

Make Your Brand Memorable.

Promotional products are more than giveaways. They’re an opportunity to connect with customers, recognize employees, strengthen relationships, and keep your brand top of mind.

At Metro Printed Products, we help companies select the right products for the right audience, occasion, and budget. From everyday essentials to premium corporate gifts, our team combines creative sourcing, competitive pricing, and dependable service to deliver merchandise that makes an impact.

What We Offer

Corporate Gifts & Recognition: Thoughtful gifts for customers, employees, executives, and business partners.
Trade Shows & Events: Branded merchandise designed to attract attention and leave a lasting impression.
Employee Appreciation: Recognition gifts, milestone awards, incentive programs, and holiday gifting.
Drinkware & Everyday Essentials: Tumblers, water bottles, office accessories, bags, and technology products.
Premium Branded Merchandise: Elevated products and recognizable retail brands for high-value gifting.
Sustainable Product Options: Environmentally conscious merchandise aligned with your company’s goals.

The Metro Advantage

Through the Proforma network, Metro has access to more than 10,000 suppliers, including preferred vendors offering End Quantity Pricing (EQP) on qualifying products. This gives our customers access to a broad selection of merchandise, multiple price points, and competitive purchasing opportunities.

We handle product research, creative recommendations, artwork coordination, production, and delivery so your team can focus on the bigger picture.
