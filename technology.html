Metro Printed Products / Why Metro

Technology That Makes Ordering Easier

Smarter Technology. Simpler Purchasing.

Managing promotional products, apparel, print, and inventory across an organization can quickly become complicated. Metro Printed Products uses technology to simplify ordering, improve purchasing visibility, and make branded merchandise programs easier to manage.

Our ProStore and ProStore Plus solutions provide centralized online ordering for approved products and programs, configurable around purchasing structures, product requirements, and fulfillment needs.

Key Benefits & Differentiators

Centralized Purchasing: Approved merchandise and consistent brand standards.
Employee Ordering: Simplified ordering and reduced manual processing.
Inventory Visibility: Reporting and inventory insights for ongoing programs.
Department Options: Purchasing options tailored to departments or locations.
Fulfillment Coordination: Integrated order processing, shipping, and support.

The Metro Advantage

A Metro company store isn’t just a place to buy products. It’s a tool for managing your branded merchandise program. Our team works behind the scenes to coordinate product availability, sourcing, order processing, inventory, and delivery.

The result: less administrative work, greater consistency, and a better ordering experience.
