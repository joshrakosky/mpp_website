Metro Printed Products / Why Metro

Woman-Owned. NWBOC-Certified. Enterprise-Ready.

Diverse Ownership. Extensive Resources. Proven Commitment.

Metro Printed Products is proud to be a woman-owned business certified by the National Women Business Owners Corporation (NWBOC). Our certification reflects our commitment to diverse business ownership while providing customers with an established partner capable of supporting sophisticated corporate purchasing and branded merchandise programs.

Key Benefits & Differentiators

Certified Woman-Owned Business: Independently recognized through NWBOC certification.
Established Experience: Serving businesses since 1990.
Extensive Supplier Access: More than 10,000 suppliers through broader sourcing resources.
Enterprise Program Capabilities: Custom online stores, inventory management, fulfillment, and coordinated purchasing.
Full-Service Solutions: Promotional products, apparel, printing, signage, packaging, and branded merchandise.
Dedicated Customer Support: Responsive account management and personalized service.

The Metro Advantage

Certified woman-owned business

Many organizations recognize the value of working with qualified diverse suppliers. Metro offers the opportunity to support supplier-diversity purchasing objectives while accessing comprehensive branded solutions through one partner.

Choosing Metro means you don’t have to sacrifice capabilities, service, or purchasing resources to work with a certified woman-owned business. We combine entrepreneurial leadership and personalized attention with the infrastructure needed to support organizations of every size.
