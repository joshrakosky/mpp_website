Metro Printed Products / Our Capabilities

Warehousing & Fulfillment

From Inventory to Delivery. We’ve Got It Covered.

Managing branded merchandise and printed materials doesn’t end when production is complete.

Metro Printed Products offers warehousing, inventory management, and fulfillment solutions that help organizations simplify operations, reduce administrative work, and deliver products where they’re needed. Whether managing recurring employee programs, multiple locations, or national distribution initiatives, Metro can help keep products organized and moving.

What We Offer

Warehousing & Storage: Organized storage for promotional products, apparel, printed materials, and program inventory.
Inventory Management: Visibility into available inventory, order activity, and replenishment needs.
Pick, Pack & Ship: Individual and bulk order processing for employees, customers, events, and corporate locations.
Multi-Location Distribution: Coordinated shipping to offices, facilities, events, and recipients across the country.
International Fulfillment: Support for international shipping documentation and delivery requirements.
Program Reporting: Inventory and order reports that support purchasing decisions and planning.
Replenishment Coordination: Monitoring inventory levels and assisting with reorders.

The Metro Advantage

Metro’s warehousing and fulfillment services can work directly with your custom e-commerce store. When an order is placed, available inventory can be allocated, processed, packed, and shipped through a coordinated workflow, reducing manual communication and improving visibility.

With established fulfillment operations and a dedicated e-commerce support team, Metro can manage the process from production through final delivery. One partner coordinates merchandise, inventory, orders, and shipping.
