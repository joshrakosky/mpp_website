Custom E-Commerce Stores

Your Brand. Your Internal Store. Simplified Ordering.

Metro Printed Products develops custom-branded internal e-commerce stores that simplify ordering, maintain brand consistency, and streamline purchasing across departments and locations. Powered by Proforma's proprietary ProStore technology, we can create individual stores for specific needs or one comprehensive store combining all our capabilities into a single platform. Each solution is customized to fit your organization.

What We Offer

Employee Apparel Stores: Easy ordering for uniforms, corporate apparel, and accessories.
Promotional Product Stores: Centralized access to approved branded merchandise.
Print Ordering Portals: Simplified ordering for business cards, marketing materials, and recurring print needs.
Inventory-Based Stores: Access to stocked products with inventory tracking and replenishment.
On-Demand Programs: Products made as ordered, reducing upfront inventory, with minimums as low as one on select items.
Department & Location Programs: Customized ordering for multiple business units, offices, and locations.

The Metro Advantage

Depending on your needs, our custom stores can include approved product catalogs, brand controls, user permissions, department-specific selections, inventory tracking, low-stock alerts, order reporting, and flexible payment options. We also support domestic and international fulfillment, along with integration planning for enterprise purchasing systems.

One Partner. One Platform. Complete Program Management.

From store development and product sourcing to inventory management, order processing, and fulfillment, Metro handles the details so your team can focus on what matters most.

We don’t simply build a store and hand it over. Our team supports store development, sourcing, inventory coordination, order processing, fulfillment, and ongoing program management. The result is a more efficient purchasing experience for employees and administrators alike.
