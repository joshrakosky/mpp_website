Apparel and Uniforms

Apparel That Represents Your Brand.

Your employees are some of your most important brand ambassadors. Whether they’re in the office, meeting customers, attending events, or working in the field, the apparel they wear should reflect the quality and professionalism of your organization.

Metro Printed Products provides branded apparel solutions for companies of every size, from individual department orders to company-wide apparel programs.

What We Offer

Corporate Polos & Dress Shirts: Professional apparel for the office, sales teams, and customer-facing employees.
Quarter-Zips & Outerwear: Premium layering pieces, fleece, softshell jackets, and performance apparel.
T-Shirts & Casual Apparel: Everyday branded clothing for employees, events, and promotions.
Headwear & Accessories: Embroidered hats, beanies, and other branded accessories.
Uniform & Team Programs: Coordinated apparel for departments, locations, and employee groups.
Premium Retail Brands: Recognizable apparel brands across a wide range of styles and price points.
Decoration Services: Embroidery, screen printing, direct-to-garment (DTG), direct-to-film (DTF), dye sublimation, heat transfer vinyl, and appliqué methods.

The Metro Advantage

Metro makes managing corporate apparel programs simple by offering consistent, brand-approved selections while maintaining flexibility for different departments, budgets, and employee preferences. Through our custom e-commerce stores, employees can easily order approved apparel, reduce administrative work and ensuring brand consistency. We also coordinate men’s and women’s companion styles, seasonal collections, new-hire apparel, and ongoing replenishment programs. With minimum order quantities (MOQs) as low as one piece on selected products, we provide the flexibility to order exactly what you need, whether it's a single item or a company-wide apparel program.

We combine extensive apparel sourcing with decoration expertise, online ordering, inventory management, and fulfillment. Whether you need 25 polos for an event or a company-wide apparel program serving thousands of employees, Metro can develop a solution around your needs.
