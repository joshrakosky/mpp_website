Our Team & Commitment

Great Service Starts with Great People.

Behind every successful project at Metro Printed Products is a dedicated team that genuinely cares about our customers and their success.

Our people bring experience, creativity, product knowledge, and a commitment to doing things the right way. We work together across account management, sourcing, graphics, e-commerce, order processing, and fulfillment to provide a coordinated and dependable customer experience.

Our Commitment to Every Customer

We Respond. Your time matters. We prioritize prompt communication, timely acknowledgments, and keeping you informed throughout the process.
We Listen. We take the time to understand your goals, challenges, budgets, and deadlines before recommending solutions.
We Take Ownership. When you trust Metro with a project, we take responsibility for managing the details and following through on our commitments.
We Work as One Team. Our departments collaborate behind the scenes to make the experience as seamless as possible for our customers.
We Build Relationships. We value long-term partnerships over individual transactions. Earning your trust and maintaining it is what drives us.

Our Promise Is Simple

We know our customers have choices. That's why we never take their business or their trust for granted.

As Metro continues to grow, our commitment remains the same: provide exceptional service, communicate openly, deliver on our promises, and always look for ways to make our customers' jobs easier.

We want every customer to feel confident knowing they have an experienced, responsive team working on their behalf.

Real People. Real Accountability. Exceptional Service.
