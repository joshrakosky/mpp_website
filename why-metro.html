Why Choose Metro

Experience. Resources. Service That Sets Us Apart.

At Metro Printed Products, we believe choosing the right partner is about more than products and pricing. It's about working with a team you can trust to deliver creative solutions, dependable execution, and exceptional service.

Since 1990, we've continued to grow through innovation, strategic acquisitions, and long-standing customer relationships. While our resources and capabilities have expanded, our commitment to personalized service has never changed.

What Makes Metro Different

Service Comes First. We believe responsiveness, communication, and accountability are the foundation of every successful partnership. Our customers deserve timely answers, clear expectations, and a team that follows through.
Experience You Can Trust. With more than 35 years in business, we understand the importance of quality, consistency, deadlines, and delivering on our commitments.
The Power of Our Network. We combine personalized service with the strength of an extensive supplier network, competitive purchasing power, and advanced technology resources to deliver exceptional value and solutions for our customers.
Solutions Built Around You. Every organization is different. We take the time to understand your needs and develop solutions that simplify purchasing, strengthen brand consistency, and support your business objectives.
One Partner. Complete Accountability. From initial ideas through final delivery, our team coordinates the details, manages the process, and takes ownership of the results.

The Metro Difference

Our greatest advantage isn't simply what we can provide. It's how we take care of our customers and our ability to offer our EQP program to all of our customers.
