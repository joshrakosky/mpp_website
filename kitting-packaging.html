Metro Printed Products / Our Capabilities

Kitting & Packaging

Create an Experience, Not Just a Package.

The way a gift or product is presented can be just as important as what’s inside.

Metro Printed Products creates custom kitting and packaging solutions that transform branded merchandise into thoughtful, memorable experiences. From employee onboarding to executive gifting and nationwide events, we help companies bring multiple products together into one coordinated package.

What We Offer

New-Hire Welcome Kits: Branded apparel, drinkware, notebooks, printed materials, and other essentials.
Employee Recognition Kits: Milestone gifts, anniversary celebrations, appreciation packages, and incentive programs.
Executive & Client Gifts: Premium merchandise presented in custom packaging.
Event & Conference Kits: Coordinated materials for trade shows, meetings, launches, and corporate events.
Sales & Marketing Kits: Product samples, promotional materials, printed collateral, and branded presentations.
Custom Packaging: Branded boxes, inserts, tissue, sleeves, and other presentation elements.
Individual & Bulk Fulfillment: Shipping to employees, customers, offices, or event locations.

The Metro Advantage

Our team can coordinate product selection and creative development; packaging design and artwork; sourcing and production; inventory receiving and storage; kit assembly and quality coordination; and shipping and distribution.

Rather than coordinating multiple suppliers, decorators, printers, and fulfillment providers, Metro brings the entire process together to deliver a consistent branded experience.
