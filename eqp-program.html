End Quantity Pricing (EQP) Program

Order Less. Save More.

Better Pricing Without Buying in Bulk.

Why pay more per item simply because you don't need a large quantity?

At Metro Printed Products, our End Quantity Pricing (EQP) Program gives customers access to preferred pricing on qualifying promotional products, even when ordering the minimum required quantity.

Instead of paying the higher per-piece price normally associated with smaller orders, eligible products receive the lowest published quantity-tier price, helping you maximize your budget without purchasing unnecessary inventory.

The Metro Advantage

Through our extensive supplier network, Metro offers access to a wide selection of EQP-eligible promotional products. The program can help organizations:

Reduce per-item costs on qualifying merchandise.
Purchase smaller quantities without sacrificing preferred pricing.
Minimize excess inventory and unnecessary spending.
Stretch budgets for promotional, marketing, and employee recognition programs.
Maintain flexibility across departments, locations, and events.

Put Your Purchasing Power to Work.

Whether you're ordering promotional giveaways, employee gifts, or premium branded merchandise, Metro can help identify EQP products that deliver greater value.
